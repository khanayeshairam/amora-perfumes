La Rinascita dei Fiori • Our Origin Story

How We Brought Wilted Flowers Back to Life

After an autumn storm swept through the historic walled gardens of Florence in 1924, thousands of Centifolia roses, Tuscan irises, and orange blossoms lay bowed and wilted in the damp earth. Where others saw a lost harvest, our founder discovered an extraordinary botanical secret: just as a flower begins to wilt, it releases its richest, most concentrated essential oils in a final sigh of devotion.

By gathering those softened petals at dusk and bathing them in warm Florentine olive macerations and slow copper-alembic steam, we awakened their dormant aroma. Every drop of Amora resurrects those faded blossoms—giving wilted flowers a second, immortal life upon the skin.

“We do not pluck flowers at their pride; we rescue them at their twilight and teach them to bloom forever.”
STAGE I • RACCOLTA AL CREPUSCOLO

Twilight Petal Rescue

Instead of discarding drooping blossoms from Florentine florists and hillside orchards, we collect wilted roses, figs, and irises at the exact moment their petals soften and deepen in nectar.

STAGE II • INFUSIONE BOTANICA

Cold Enfleurage & Warm Maceration

The wilted petals are pressed onto purified botanical waxes and steeped in warm amber resin, drawing out the hidden aromatic soul locked inside each curled leaf.

STAGE III • RINASCITA IN FLACONE

Rebirth in Crystal Flacons

Aged for ninety days in darkened cellars until the scent blooms anew—transforming once-wilted flowers into radiant, long-lasting extraits de parfum.
